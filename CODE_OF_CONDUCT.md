# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and safety-conscious. Accept feedback gracefully, focus criticism on
ideas and code, and never pressure others to perform unsafe hardware experiments. Harassment,
trolling, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, and publication of private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban participants whose behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report incidents privately to the
maintainers listed in the repository security contacts. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
